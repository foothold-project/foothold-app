# Asset attribution

- Collapsed House by Nate Koenig / OpenRobotics: https://fuel.gazebosim.org/1.0/OpenRobotics/models/Collapsed%20house — Creative Commons Zero v1.0 Universal (CC0-1.0), https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Collapsed industrial building by Nate Koenig / OpenRobotics: https://fuel.gazebosim.org/1.0/OpenRobotics/models/Collapsed%20industrial%20building — Creative Commons Zero v1.0 Universal (CC0-1.0), confirmed from the Fuel model API2026-10-30; same license URL above.

`house-albedo.png` is a512px derivative of the original house albedo. `stone-albedo.png` and `rubble-albedo.png` are256px derivatives of the industrial-building rough concrete and rubble albedos used in the10-23 Gazebo source. `wood-albedo.png` is a256px derivative of the locally generated10-23 wood texture. Original UV intent, metre-scale tiling, and SDF tints are retained; source-resolution texture files are not included.

Rubble, boards, concrete and dust geometry are locally generated. Exterior fragments preserve the selected10-23 pieces' XY footprints and are re-seated below0.8m; interior route/steps follow the10-29 layout. All rubble is fixed. Small-chip and exterior contacts sample the visible rubble at 2.5cm spacing (`perimeter_contact_*.png` tiles); this removes broad boxes bridging separate rocks or extending beyond the porch, but fills space beneath boards and interpolates narrow edges. Low upward house faces use 4cm thick convex slabs with the original top and footprint; concave patches remain split. Roofs and high furniture remain visual only, and walls retain the body-height band approximation.

Regenerate only these mobile assets with `python3 -m apps.simmgr.tools.import_house <local-source> --mobile-only`; this leaves server/Gazebo assets unchanged. `height.png` follows the displayed dust/porch triangles. `map.json` uses the same sampled contacts, with entry steps retained as traversal candidates. The visual scene and top image do not change when only collisions change. `FH_SIM_SRC=<local-dependencies> node mobile/scripts/house-collision-selftest.mjs` checks the four reported support gaps, the entrance route, and exterior contact excess against the visible surface across a 10cm world grid. Optional `--dynamics` records simulation evidence without assessing walking success.
